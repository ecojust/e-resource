# Bund09 visible-detail-v2

This independent revision addresses three concrete omissions found by comparing the preserved `glass-inset-v1` Blender preview with the original P01 photograph. The baseline raw SHA is `de7fdbd4bb3f4b66e0f506aed39b4412a05481c610bea8607d352ebe4971dce6`. Its raw, compressed native, actual reopen evidence and appearance review remain in the sibling `bund09` stage. The baseline appearance review did **not** claim photographic fidelity passed.

The inspected source is Suicasmo's photograph of 2025-05-01, CC0, [Commons source page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:China_Merchants_Building,_Shanghai_20250501.jpg). Its original 5184 × 3456 JPEG remains byte-identical. Four unannotated inspection crops are recorded, with original pixel bounds and hashes, in `references/manifest.json`.

The source footprint, three-floor arrangement, 12 column positions at each upper level, differing column orders, seven rear arches per upper floor, two principal pediments, short wing, entrance lettering, all actual openings, every glass pane and the registered photo UVs are retained. The original photograph has dark recessed gallery windows; this revision does not brighten all shaded glazing.

`check_visible_detail_revision.py` compares the actual raw GLBs. It requires every attribute and index in all unchanged material batches to remain exactly equal, including glass, frames, brick, lettering and the inferred inner roof cap. Within the shared pale-stone material it additionally requires every old triangle outside a small, declared central-balcony box to survive with exact FLOAT32 positions, normals and multiplicity. Four stone-arch partition checks verify disjoint stone/joint areas. These checks establish the permitted geometry scope; they do not establish photographic fidelity.

`output/bund09-all-parts-check.json` checks each generated solid after FLOAT32 welding for zero collapsed triangles, closed edges, paired winding and positive signed volume. Roof support sheets are explicitly identified as sheets. This is not a whole-building Boolean-union claim. Final native rendering and a new photo/preview review are performed separately by the root task.

The ordinary Python build uses the unchanged shared helper. A local sub-nanometre simplification removes redundant collinear points in the new stone partitions, with zero area change; no footprint or opening is simplified. No source photograph or UV is resampled, rotated or recolored.
