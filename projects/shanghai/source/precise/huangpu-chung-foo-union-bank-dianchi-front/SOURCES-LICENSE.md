# Reference photography and rebuilding the geometry

The closely framed photograph *Zhongfudalou* by Fayhoo identifies the bank at 103 Dianchi Road. Its recorded upload date is 19 January 2015. The measured north elevation, column-head outlines and derived architectural models are shared under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0. Retain the author's name, original file record, licence and notice of reconstruction when redistributing the models. [Photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Zhongfudalou.jpg) · [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)

The architectural archive labels the separate oblique photograph “立面20170415”. It establishes spatial relationships at the street and records parts outside the closer photograph. The archive image was used for comparing architectural dimensions. The distributed sources contain measurements, camera estimates and newly generated three-dimensional geometry. [Archive record and checksums](measurements/street-observations.json)
