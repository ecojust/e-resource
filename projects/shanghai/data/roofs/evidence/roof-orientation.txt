OSM Tag History
roof:orientation
specifies the orientation of a roof shape specified with
roof:shape
=*
. This tag describes the direction of the roof ridge, not the roof surface(s) themselves. In most buildings, this roof ridge runs parallel to the longer of two building edges, which means
along
.
In contrast, the alternative
roof:direction
=*
describes the orientation of the roof surface(s) itself. In the case of pent roofs (
skillion
) and square buildings, capturing with
roof:orientation
is not suitable;
roof:direction
must be used here. These two tags are alternatives, it is sufficient to use one of them.
